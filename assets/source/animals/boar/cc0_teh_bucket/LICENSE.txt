Eco Rebirth modifications
-------------------------

The source is retained as a public-domain anatomy and motion reference. Its
legacy 313-vertex mesh is not shipped as the gameplay surface. Eco Rebirth
authors a new continuous adult wild-boar body, exact 22-bone runtime rig, PBR
bristle surface, split hooves, tusks, articulated actions and gameplay sockets.

The original source and Eco Rebirth modifications in this directory may be
used under CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required; this notice is retained for
asset provenance and reproducible builds.
