The production GLBs are generated by
`tools/blender/build_cinematic_otter.py`. They retain the project's 22-bone
runtime contract while rebuilding the adult river otter's low streamlined
chest, compact rounded head, short limbs, full webbed feet and broad rudder
tail. The reference is used only to cross-check silhouette and proportion.
