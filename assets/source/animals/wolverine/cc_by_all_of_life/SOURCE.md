The production GLBs are generated by
`tools/blender/build_cinematic_wolverine.py`. They retain the project's
22-bone runtime contract while rebuilding an adult wolverine's low stocky
mustelid body, broad head, rounded ears, pale shoulder saddle, powerful paws,
long claws and thick tapered tail. The linked model is used only to cross-check
silhouette and proportion.
