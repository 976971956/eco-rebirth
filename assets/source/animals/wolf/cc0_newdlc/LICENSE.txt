Eco Rebirth modifications
-------------------------

- Repainted the original albedo into a gray-wolf palette with OpenAI image
  generation while preserving the source UV layout.
- Rebuilt the PBR material from the supplied albedo, normal and roughness maps.
- Reduced the runtime skeleton to 32 weighted bones by merging terminal toe
  weights.
- Added eight original gameplay animation clips and Godot skill sockets.
- Generated separate Hero and Mobile GLB exports for Godot 4 Compatibility.
