Eco Rebirth modifications
-------------------------

The source is retained as a public-domain crocodilian anatomy, scale texture
and motion reference. Its 292-vertex production mesh is not shipped as the
gameplay surface. Eco Rebirth authors a new continuous adult marsh-crocodile
surface, exact 22-bone runtime rig, broad articulated jaws, scale PBR material,
three rows of osteoderms, splayed feet and distinct crawl/swim/death-roll
actions.

The original source and Eco Rebirth modifications in this directory may be
used under CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required; this notice is retained for
asset provenance and reproducible builds.
