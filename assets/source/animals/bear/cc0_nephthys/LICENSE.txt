Eco Rebirth modifications
-------------------------

The source is retained as a public-domain shape and palette reference. Its
upright toy topology is not shipped as the gameplay mesh. Eco Rebirth authors
a new quadrupedal adult brown-bear surface, exact 22-bone runtime rig, PBR fur,
plantigrade feet, articulated actions, facial details, and gameplay sockets.

The original source and Eco Rebirth modifications in this directory may be
used under CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required; this notice is retained for
asset provenance and reproducible builds.
