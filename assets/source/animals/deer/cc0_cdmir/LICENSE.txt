Eco Rebirth modifications
-------------------------

- Rebuilt the legacy materials from the two packed 1024 body/head fur maps as
  Godot Compatibility PBR materials.
- Reduced the 31-bone source control rig to the 22-bone runtime ground-animal
  contract while retaining three independently articulated limb segments.
- Added an original lightweight adult-stag antler silhouette, eight gameplay
  clips, and two Godot skill sockets.
- Added separate Hero and Mobile subdivision, texture, and antler profiles.
