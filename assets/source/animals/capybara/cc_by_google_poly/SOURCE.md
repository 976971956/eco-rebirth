- Source listing: 864 triangles, OBJ/GLTF
- Access checked: 2026-08-16

The production GLBs are not a repack of the reference. They are generated from
`tools/blender/build_cinematic_capybara.py`, using an original continuous
barrel-bodied mesh, the project's 22-bone runtime contract, species-specific
short plantigrade limbs, partially webbed feet and authored animations. The
reference is used to cross-check the adult capybara's blunt high-set head,
nearly invisible tail, heavy rump and low, calm silhouette.
