Eco Rebirth modifications
-------------------------

The source is retained as a public-domain silhouette, markings and motion
reference. Its cubic production mesh is not shipped as the gameplay surface.
Eco Rebirth authors a new continuous adult-raccoon body, exact 22-bone runtime
rig, PBR fur, articulated grasping paws, facial mask, ring tail, gameplay
actions and skill sockets.

The original source and Eco Rebirth modifications in this directory may be
used under CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required; this notice is retained for
asset provenance and reproducible builds.
