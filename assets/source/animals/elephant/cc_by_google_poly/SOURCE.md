# Elephant reference provenance

- Title: `Elephant`
- Creator: Poly by Google
- Source: https://poly.pizza/m/a27MA0rXyyj
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported
- Published: 2017-09-25
- Source listing: approximately 1.1k triangles / 2.23k vertices, OBJ/GLTF
- Access checked: 2026-08-16

The production GLBs are generated by
`tools/blender/build_cinematic_elephant.py`. They retain the project's 22-bone
runtime contract while rebuilding an adult African elephant's deep barrel,
high shoulders, broad fan ears, columnar articulated legs, round foot pads,
curved tusks and a continuously skinned tapering trunk. The linked model is
used only to cross-check silhouette and proportion.
