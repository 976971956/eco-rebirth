The files in this directory are the archived source glTF, binary buffer and
base-color texture. They are retained as an anatomy, silhouette and quill-flow
reference. Eco Rebirth's production porcupine is a newly authored derivative
with a new continuous body, runtime rig, materials and animation set.

Changes: rebuilt proportions and topology; added three-segment legs, a 22-bone
runtime rig, PBR surface treatment, layered quills, facial details, skill
sockets and eight gameplay animations; generated separate Hero and Mobile
profiles.
