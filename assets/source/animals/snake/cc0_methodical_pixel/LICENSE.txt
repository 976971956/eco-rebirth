Eco Rebirth modifications
-------------------------

- Preserved the single connected source mesh, authored UV layout, grounded
  serpentine silhouette, and original CC0 provenance.
- Rebuilt the 206-triangle cage into separate Hero/Mobile Catmull-Clark
  profiles and remapped it to the project's ten-bone long-body rig.
- Recoloured the authored pattern into a blue-green forest scale set and
  generated albedo, normal, and roughness PBR textures from the source mask.
- Added original eyes, nostrils, jaw, fangs, forked tongue, two gameplay
  sockets, and eight baked gameplay actions.

The original source and Eco Rebirth modifications in this directory may be
used under CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required; this notice is retained for
asset provenance and reproducible builds.
