# Snowy owl reference provenance

- Title: `Owl`
- Creator: Poly by Google
- Source: https://poly.pizza/m/eoAo21aoZHJ
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported
- Published: 2017-10-22
- Source listing: approximately 1.5k triangles / 792 vertices, OBJ/GLTF
- Access checked: 2026-08-16

The production GLBs are generated by `tools/blender/build_cinematic_owl.py`.
They keep the flight controller's three-stage wing contract while rebuilding
a snowy owl's barrel chest, compact neck, broad facial disk, short hooked beak,
feathered legs, talons, fan tail and white plumage with flush grey barring. The
linked model is used only to cross-check raptor silhouette and proportion.
