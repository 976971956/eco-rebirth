# Tiger reference provenance

- Title: `Tiger`
- Creator: Poly by Google
- Source: https://poly.pizza/m/5A3w06FXUup
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported
- Published: 2017-10-22
- Source listing: 742 triangles / approximately 3.38k vertices, OBJ/GLTF
- Access checked: 2026-08-16

The production GLBs are generated by `tools/blender/build_cinematic_tiger.py`.
They retain the project's 22-bone runtime contract while rebuilding an adult
tiger's long low felid torso, strong shoulder girdle, broad paws, rounded dark
ears, white cheek ruffs, flush asymmetric body and leg stripes, and a thick
connected ringed tail. The linked model is used only to cross-check silhouette
and proportion.
