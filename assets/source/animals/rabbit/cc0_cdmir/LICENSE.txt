Eco Rebirth modifications
-------------------------

- Rebuilt the legacy Blender materials as Godot-compatible PBR materials from
  the packed diffuse and tangent-space normal textures.
- Normalized the authored rabbit proportions to the game's species scale.
- Reduced the source control rig to the 22-bone runtime ground-animal contract.
- Added eight original gameplay animation clips and two Godot skill sockets.
- Added separate Hero and Mobile geometry/texture profiles, using 512 and 256
  pixel maps respectively for the small on-screen species.
