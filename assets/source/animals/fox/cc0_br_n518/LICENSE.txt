Eco Rebirth modifications
-------------------------

- Applied the source mirror safely and rebuilt its legacy material as a
  Godot-compatible PBR coat from the original UV diffuse and normal maps.
- Corrected head/tail length, rib-cage mass, waist, haunches and brush-tail
  volume while preserving the authored connected topology.
- Retargeted the source weights to the project's 22-bone runtime contract.
- Added eight original gameplay animation clips and two Godot skill sockets.
- Added separate Hero and Mobile subdivision and texture profiles.
