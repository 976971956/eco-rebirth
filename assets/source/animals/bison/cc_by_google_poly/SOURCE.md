# Bison reference provenance

- Title: `Bison`
- Creator: Poly by Google
- Source: https://poly.pizza/m/30FwRBwJ-rC
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported
- Published: 2017-10-21
- Source listing: approximately 1k triangles / 534 vertices, OBJ/GLTF
- Access checked: 2026-08-16

The production GLBs are generated by
`tools/blender/build_cinematic_bison.py`. They retain the project's 22-bone
runtime contract while rebuilding an adult American bison's massive shoulder
hump, low broad forehead, compact rear quarters, short swept horns, chest
beard, foreleg fringe and heavy split hooves. The linked model is used only to
cross-check silhouette and proportion.
